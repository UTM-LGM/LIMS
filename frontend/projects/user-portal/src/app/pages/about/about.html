Who We Are

The Technical Services Division of the Malaysian Rubber Board (MRB) provides independent, internationally recognized laboratory testing, calibration, product certification, and technical consultancy to manufacturers, exporters, and regulatory bodies worldwide.

Through this Integrated Technical Services Portal, customers can conveniently browse test scopes, generate instant quotations, submit out-of-scope inquiries, and track sample testing progress from submission to certified digital report issuance.
